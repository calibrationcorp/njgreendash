Case Study & Consulting Narrative

Consulting Story
Consultant Lucian DeMarco developed this fictional Riverview Grow Complex facility scenario to illustrate an early-stage consulting engagement with a prospective operator. The study considers a 25,000 SF cultivation concept, site access, operational adjacencies and a coordinated feasibility approach. No real client or site is represented.
Principal Hurdles
Multiple greenhouse bays compete for service routes, stormwater area and phased utility distribution.
Proposed Planning Approach
A preliminary strategy organizes greenhouse a, greenhouse b, greenhouse c together with secured support space. Landscaping and separate circulation are evaluated as part of the hypothetical site concept.
High Points
Expandable grow bays and shared services permit an intelligible operating strategy.
Limitations / Risks
Operational utility loads increase sharply as greenhouse bays multiply.
Licensing Questions (Not Cleared)
Illustrative Class 1 cultivation review, municipal zoning, utility capacity, stormwater, and other site-specific requirements remain outstanding.
Key Takeaway
Make greenhouse phasing and shared services part of the first site sketch.
Site & Landscape
Illustrative landscaping features native planting buffers, accessible pedestrian routes, street-tree approaches, stormwater landscape concepts and screening for service areas.
