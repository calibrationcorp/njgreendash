Case Study & Consulting Narrative

Consulting Story
Consultant Lucian DeMarco developed this fictional Canopy Modular Campus facility scenario to illustrate an early-stage consulting engagement with a prospective operator. The study considers a 30,000 SF cultivation concept, site access, operational adjacencies and a coordinated feasibility approach. No real client or site is represented.
Principal Hurdles
Modular phase expansion may disrupt utility supply, vehicle access and stormwater systems if left unplanned.
Proposed Planning Approach
A preliminary strategy organizes grow module a, grow module b, grow module c together with secured support space. Landscaping and separate circulation are evaluated as part of the hypothetical site concept.
High Points
Repeatable greenhouse bays support planning for staged capital investment.
Limitations / Risks
Phased construction could interrupt existing operations.
Licensing Questions (Not Cleared)
Illustrative Class 1 cultivation review, municipal zoning, utility capacity, stormwater, and other site-specific requirements remain outstanding.
Key Takeaway
Protect circulation and utility corridors for future phases.
Site & Landscape
Illustrative landscaping features native planting buffers, accessible pedestrian routes, street-tree approaches, stormwater landscape concepts and screening for service areas.
