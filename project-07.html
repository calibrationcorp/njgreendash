Case Study & Consulting Narrative

Consulting Story
Consultant Lucian DeMarco developed this fictional Secure Distribution Hub facility scenario to illustrate an early-stage consulting engagement with a prospective operator. The study considers a 18,000 SF distribution concept, site access, operational adjacencies and a coordinated feasibility approach. No real client or site is represented.
Principal Hurdles
Receiving and dispatch traffic can intersect secured storage and staff approaches on constrained sites.
Proposed Planning Approach
A preliminary strategy organizes receiving, secure inventory, dispatch together with secured support space. Landscaping and separate circulation are evaluated as part of the hypothetical site concept.
High Points
Clear custody-route narrative and practical separation of public and secure zones.
Limitations / Risks
Dock geometry and neighbor impacts need site-specific study.
Licensing Questions (Not Cleared)
Illustrative Class 4 distribution pathway with secure custody, vehicle circulation, land-use and NJ-CRC questions still to be verified.
Key Takeaway
Test truck circulation, secure boundaries and staff routes before leasing.
Site & Landscape
Illustrative landscaping features native planting buffers, accessible pedestrian routes, street-tree approaches, stormwater landscape concepts and screening for service areas.
