Case Study & Consulting Narrative

Consulting Story
Lucian DeMarco prepared a fictional 8,200 SF Costa Sol Spanish Revival Wellness Center facility consultation scenario, exploring spanish Revival-inspired hypothetical medicinal ATC with courtyard and private patient-service zones. The concept studies space programming, permitting questions and how a preferred visual style can coexist with operational needs. No actual clients, locations or approvals are represented.
Principal Hurdles
Patient privacy and security circulation, existing medicinal license application restrictions, and specialized mechanical requirements require early coordination.
Proposed Planning Approach
The illustrative program organizes patient reception, dispensing concept, consultation rooms and support zones with a staff-only circulation spine, mechanical service considerations and a separate public approach wherever applicable.
High Points
A distinctive Spanish Revival identity supports a memorable, welcoming commercial presence.
Limitations / Risks
Style-specific envelope details can increase up-front costs and complicate duct routing, equipment access, envelope performance and construction coordination.
Licensing Questions (Not Cleared)
NJ-CRC is not currently accepting new medicinal cannabis business applications. This concept does not imply any such application or approval.
Key Takeaway
Coordinate operational rooms, HVAC and illumination criteria, fire/life-safety and authorized-worker circulation before finalizing the facade and lease.
Site & Landscape
Concept landscape includes appropriate native or low-maintenance plants, accessible arrival, buffered services, stormwater concepts and a screened loading approach.
