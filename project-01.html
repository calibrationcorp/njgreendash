Case Study & Consulting Narrative

Consulting Story
Consultant Lucian DeMarco developed this fictional Garden State Dispensary facility scenario to illustrate an early-stage consulting engagement with a prospective operator. The study considers a 8,500 SF adult-use retail concept, site access, operational adjacencies and a coordinated feasibility approach. No real client or site is represented.
Principal Hurdles
Balancing public-facing retail access with inventory custody, parking availability and a compact support wing.
Proposed Planning Approach
A preliminary strategy organizes public entry & waiting, sales & consultation, staff support together with secured support space. Landscaping and separate circulation are evaluated as part of the hypothetical site concept.
High Points
Recognizable storefront presence, landscaped approach and clear customer/employee separation.
Limitations / Risks
Back-of-house storage and local parking circulation can limit sales-area flexibility.
Licensing Questions (Not Cleared)
Illustrative Class 5 retail review, local land-use permission, site control, operational conditions, and state licensing remain outstanding.
Key Takeaway
Start the customer experience and secure goods routes together.
Site & Landscape
Illustrative landscaping features native planting buffers, accessible pedestrian routes, street-tree approaches, stormwater landscape concepts and screening for service areas.
